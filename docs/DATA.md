# Data provenance, calculations and limitations

This document describes the current data and calculation pipeline. Exact counts change when the dataset is regenerated; inspect `app/src/main/assets/pace_dataset.json` and its metadata for the current snapshot rather than relying on stale counts in prose.

## Bundled data

The Android app bundles `app/src/main/assets/pace_dataset.json`, generated by `tools/build_dataset.py`. It contains tournament metadata and season readings for two distinct metrics:

1. CPI physical readings from courtspeed.com.
2. Surface Speed values from the calibrated archive calculation (CALC) and published Tennis Abstract (TA) references/fallbacks.

Where CALC and TA overlap, CALC is primary and the TA value is preserved as `publishedSpeed`. Where no eligible CALC exists, TA remains a labelled fallback. TA is a comparison series, not physical ground truth.

## CPI

CPI combines coefficient of friction and coefficient of restitution:

```
CPI = 100(1 − μ) + 150(0.81 − e)
```

Coverage is limited to instrumented show courts and published records. It does not measure every court or every match at an event. Measurement timing, court identity and surface wear matter.

## Surface Speed

For each server side, the calculation combines the server's ace rate and the returner's ace-concession rate using calibrated odds:

```
O = ((p_server / (1-p_server)) × (p_returner / (1-p_returner)))
    / (p_tour / (1-p_tour))
expected_ace_rate = O / (1 + O)
expected_aces = service_points × expected_ace_rate
side_ratio = actual_aces / max(expected_aces, 0.5)
```

The event raw score is the mean of valid server-side ratios. A completed event receives a CALC reading as soon as at least one valid opponent-adjusted server side exists; there is no 10- or 20-side eligibility floor. The season normaliser remains anchored to the mean raw score of events with at least 20 adjusted sides when that cohort exists, so a tiny draw does not move the 1.00 tour reference for every event. If a season has no such events, all rateable events form the fallback normaliser cohort.

Historical ratings use leave-one-tournament-out player profiles and remove the target event from the current-season tour-average anchor. Player tendencies are aggregated from up to 35 prior ATP seasons beginning in 1991, plus current-season training rows; older season samples receive a 0.90 per-year recency weight. Historical player IDs and current TML IDs are crosswalked only through unique normalised full names, with ambiguous names left unmatched. Qualified clay and hard/grass rates require three matches in a surface bucket and use a 150-service-point prior toward the corresponding surface tour rate, then blend one third to two thirds. When no surface-qualified estimate exists, pooled career serve/return samples are shrunk toward the pooled tour rate. A truly missing personal rate is finally filled with the current leave-one-event-out tour-average rate; this is recorded as `tour-average-prior-only`, not misrepresented as an individual player profile.

Live ratings use the same expected-aces equation, the expanded multi-season profile database and the same explicit neutral-prior policy. A fresh live manifest excludes active events from current-season training. The baseline stores the history window, recency factor, per-role profile source, sample counts and tour-prior fallback counts. If the matching baseline is unavailable or the season tour-average rate is invalid, the app withholds the rating rather than silently using an uncalibrated method.

The expected-aces floor of 0.5 protects the actual/expected ratio from very small denominators. It does not exclude a mathematically valid server side from coverage.

Davis Cup is a separate target event in Surface Speed. The historical calculator accepts Davis Cup (`D`) rows with valid singles aces and service-point counts, groups separate tie labels into one annual Davis Cup rating, and uses the standard ATP player-profile evidence and season anchor. Davis Cup rows are excluded from ATP profile training and from the ATP normaliser. The underlying historical archive has usable serve-stat fields in 2016–2024; seasons without valid source match statistics remain unrated rather than being filled with TA values or estimates. The annual competition spans multiple venues and surfaces, so the edition is labelled multi-surface rather than assigned to one court type.

For 2026, Davis Cup is enabled as a separate target only when the TML annual ATP match CSV and `ongoing_tourneys.csv` are both fetched or restored from fresh, hash-verified caches and their schemas pass validation. The 2026 source identity is recorded as `TennisMyLife ATP Match Database + ongoing_tourneys.csv`; an ATP archive fallback or annual TML file without the ongoing supplement cannot publish a 2026 Davis Cup CALC. Historical 2016–2024 editions retain their previously validated source window.

## Live match population and parsing

The app discovers standard-format Grand Slams, ATP Finals, Masters 1000, ATP 500 and ATP 250 events from ATP current-scores/results pages on-device. Next Gen ATP Finals is also admitted as an explicit format-specific target, without admitting unrelated special events. It reads singles match links and distinguishes completed archive links from live/in-progress links. Result-card markers identify walkovers and retirements generically, not through tournament-specific ID lists. Next Gen matches are rated against standard ATP player profiles and the standard season anchor, but do not enter standard profile training or normalisation; its CALC remains format-specific.

- Played completed singles with valid statistics are eligible. Both historical CALC and live ratings are published as soon as at least one server-side observation can be opponent-adjusted; neither has a minimum match-count or adjusted-side eligibility floor.
- Completed CALC events below the 20-side stable-sample cohort remain ranked, but are labelled with their match and adjusted-side counts. Their small samples do not enter the season normaliser when a 20-side cohort exists. For example, the refreshed 2026 Laver Cup row is CALC 0.47 from seven matches and 14 adjusted sides, with Tennis Abstract's 0.44 retained alongside it.
- Live samples below eight completed matches are labelled as early samples in the Live now row. This is a caution label only, not a calculation gate.
- Match-cluster confidence intervals are only emitted when at least five independent completed-match clusters are available; very small samples still receive a CALC point estimate but not a misleading degenerate interval.
- Retirements with played-set statistics are eligible.
- Walkovers and confirmed no-play matches are excluded.
- Doubles and in-progress matches are excluded.
- Full-match aggregate stats are preferred. When incomplete, valid played-set arrays are summed; malformed or incomplete played sets remain unresolved.
- Unavailable stats remain visible as missing. The app must not invent values.
- Cache entries are intersected with the current draw so stale match IDs do not contribute.

ATP/Infosys CDN access is device-sensitive. Successful CI verifies code and tests, not necessarily live retrieval from a physical handset. Verify coverage on-device.

## Live metadata and runtime rating path

`data/live.json` is the source event-window file. The dataset builder intentionally carries all known windows into `liveSchedule`, including ended events, so the Android client can reject stale routes still present on the ATP current-scores hub. Only started, non-expired, non-terminal windows are copied into the dataset's visible `live` fallback list; future windows remain in `liveSchedule` until their start dates. An ended row can therefore be present in `liveSchedule` without being an active event. Keep its tournament ID and date bounds until the stale-route guard no longer needs them; update its status to a terminal value rather than deleting the schedule row.

The three-hour `refresh-live.yml` workflow is a **dataset snapshot publisher**, not a live-rating calculator. It rebuilds the app dataset and copies the result to `data/latest.json`. Its ATP page probe is diagnostic only. `tools/live_update.py` is a legacy experimental updater; it is not invoked by this workflow or by the app and its old 20-match gate/formula must not be used as the current production method.

At runtime, `AppViewModel` asks `AtpLiveClient` to discover active standard-tour events plus the explicit Next Gen ATP Finals target. The Android client obtains completed singles links from ATP results pages, retrieves match statistics via `AtpInfosysStatsClient`, and calculates live Surface Speed using the current `AceRating` method and the season baseline included in the published dataset. Per-event results are cached locally. A `null` speed in `data/live.json` is expected for this architecture and is not evidence that the app's device-side Infosys path is unused.

| Use | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|

| Behavioural reference | Tennis Abstract | Published rolling reference values |
| Historical match stats | Jeff Sackmann ATP archive mirror | Historical tour-level match data |

| Live match stats | ATP/Infosys Stats Plus | Runtime read-only requests from Android |

The current-season pipeline validates the TML annual CSV and Davis Cup supplement schemas and records URL, fetch timestamp, row count, byte count and SHA-256 in sidecar manifests. A cached snapshot is used only after refresh failure and only if its manifest/hash match and it is no older than seven days. The 2026 Davis Cup source identity is recorded as `TennisMyLife ATP Match Database + ongoing_tourneys.csv`; the dataset builder checks that identity before publishing a 2026 Davis Cup CALC.

TennisMyLife's [published repository terms](https://github.com/Tennismylife/TML-Database) limit use to personal, educational and non-commercial research; they do not authorize raw CSV redistribution/resale or commercial use. The Davis Cup integration is restricted to a free non-commercial analytical feature: raw TML rows remain transient in memory/build cache, are not committed or bundled, and the app displays only an aggregate event-level rating and coverage counts. Do not add ads, paid tiers, commercial model services or raw/match-level exports without prior written permission. TML is not an official ATP/ITF feed, and it cannot grant rights held by third parties; review those rights separately. See `DATA-LICENSES.md`.

## Current audit evidence

The latest completed formula comparison covered 1,289 common event-seasons across 36 seasons (1991–2026), using 5,000 season-stratified paired bootstrap resamples. The calibrated surface-returner variant scored Pearson 0.925, Spearman 0.926 and MAE 0.052 against the Tennis Abstract reference. This is evidence of agreement with that reference, not physical validation.

The 150-service-point shrinkage configuration improved historical out-of-sample reference agreement over the unshrunk configuration. Full-pipeline event bootstrap intervals include player-profile, target-event and season-normaliser uncertainty and are wider than conditional match-only intervals.

The full all-event profile-coverage audit swept 1991–2026:

| Measure | Result |
|---|---:|
| Seasons | 36 |
| Mapped event-seasons | 1,533 |
| Eligible matches | 71,150 |
| Eligible server sides | 142,300 |
| Rateable server sides | 142,300 |
| Unrateable server sides | 0 |
| Sides using at least one neutral tour-average prior | 1,930 (1.356%) |

All sides received finite calculation inputs, but a neutral prior is not the same thing as an individual player's serve/return history. The corrected 1991–2026 sweep records 1,930 sides using at least one prior (1.356%), with 980 server-role and 980 returner-role uses. Those 1,960 role uses exceed the side count because 30 sides required a prior for both roles. The 1,610 unique missing player-role assignments (805 server-role, 805 returner-role) were all due to no player profile in the selected history window; there were zero ambiguous normalized-name collisions and zero existing profiles without a usable role rate. Reports omit player names and IDs.

Montpellier 2026 production full-history CALC is 1.664 (rank 1/57), compared with TA's published 1.49; the conditional match-cluster 95% interval is [1.465, 1.862]. Marrakech 2026 production CALC is 0.999 (rank 34/57), compared with TA's 0.93; its conditional interval is [0.828, 1.182]. Both TA reference points fall inside the corresponding production intervals. The separate \`deep_calculation_audit.py\` variant/bootstrap results (Montpellier 1.600 at 47 sides; Marrakech 1.049 at 41 sides) use same-season-only profiles and are not the production 35-season profile method.

Montpellier's source ace rate remains 14.2% versus TA's published 19.8%; this TML audit does not establish TA's match population or statistic definition. ## Independent physical validation

The ace-based metric is behavioural. Independent validation should use ITF Court Pace Rating (CPR) or properly sourced CPI readings, with event/season/court identity, measurement date, source, surface construction, temperature, humidity, altitude and ball model where available.

Use event-cluster bootstrap intervals, surface-stratified analysis, within-surface association and held-out events/seasons. Do not treat several courts from one tournament as independent events. Do not infer missing environmental or court-specific values.

See [PHYSICAL_VALIDATION.md](PHYSICAL_VALIDATION.md) for the schema and validation protocol.

## Automatic refresh and calendar-year policy

The downloadable dataset consumed by the Android app is `data/latest.json`; the same payload is bundled as `app/src/main/assets/pace_dataset.json`.

- On app startup, the app checks the published dataset. While the app remains open, it rechecks that dataset every 60 minutes and keeps the selected season where possible.
- While the app process is alive, the ATP current-scores hub is rechecked every five minutes for standard-format main-tour events: Grand Slams, ATP Finals, Masters 1000, ATP 500 and ATP 250. Special formats such as Next Gen remain outside this standard live formula. A readable current hub is authoritative for the active slate. If the hub cannot be read, dated active fallback rows are used.
- The dataset's `live` array is the current display list. Its separate `liveSchedule` array retains known event windows, including ended events, so a results route left behind on the ATP hub cannot revive a completed tournament. The app checks the scheduled end date and terminal status before accepting a discovered route. Recently completed standard-tour events are separately backfilled for seven days into the season rating board without reappearing in Live now. Locally cached live ratings remain season readings after the event window closes. Live-row progress is keyed by tournament ID, not shared globally between events.
- The source-generation workflow backfills every missing Tennis Abstract calendar-year page in the configured range (1991-current in the repository workflow), then rechecks the previous and current year pages on routine refreshes. Missing or empty historical years are retried; a page with fewer than 10 mapped event rows is not used to replace a last-known season. The workflow also refreshes the Court Speed CPI table. It is scheduled every six hours and can also be run manually. Once the workflow is on the default branch, GitHub activates that schedule; schedules on an unmerged PR branch are not active.
- Tennis Abstract's year selector is the season key. The selected year, not the tournament's first date, is stored as `s`. This preserves events such as 2025 Hong Kong/Brisbane/United Cup that have December 2024 start dates, while placing 2025 and 2026 Laver Cup in separate season rows. It is not the rolling last-52-weeks view.
- Historical TA snapshots are revisited only when a previously-unmapped source label becomes resolvable through a new crosswalk alias. This keeps alias improvements (for example, the historical "Masters Cup" label for the ATP Finals) from requiring a full 36-year refetch on every refresh; after the alias is mapped, that season returns to the stable-snapshot policy.
- Next Gen Finals receives its own CALC target rating as well as the published Tennis Abstract reference; TA includes it in the same ace-rate-adjusted surface-speed table. Because Next Gen uses short first-to-four sets, best-of-five format and no-ad scoring, its matches are excluded from standard ATP player-profile fitting and the standard ATP season normaliser, but they are rated against those standard ATP profiles and that standard anchor. Its format-specific CALC is shown without the general 2.0 ceiling, labelled with a format caveat, and does not change any standard-event rating or baseline. The generated history has Next Gen 2024 CALC 2.146 (15 matches / 30 adjusted sides; conditional interval [1.659, 2.669]) and 2025 CALC 1.303 (15 / 30; [1.009, 1.690]). Interpret these separately from standard-format events. TA's 2025 calendar-year selector displays 0.81, while its rolling 52-week view updated 9 November 2026 displays 1.26 for the same edition; the history builder retains the year-selector snapshot for reproducibility. Sources: [Tennis Abstract 2025 year selector](https://www.tennisabstract.com/cgi-bin/surface-speed.cgi?year=2025), [Tennis Abstract rolling 52-week report](https://tennisabstract.com/reports/atp_surface_speed.html), [ATP Next Gen rules](https://www.atptour.com/en/news/next-gen-atp-finals-presented-by-pif-2024-rules-and-innovations) and [Next Gen ATP Finals format](https://www.nextgenatpfinals.com/en/event/format).
- Host cities for travelling events are attached to each season reading, not frozen in the tournament catalogue. Laver Cup venue metadata covers Prague 2017, Chicago 2018, Geneva 2019, Boston 2021, London 2022, Vancouver 2023, Berlin 2024, San Francisco 2025 and London 2026; 2020 was not held. The app resolves the edition-specific city on both the Speed board and tournament detail screen. The catalogue's default Laver Cup city is the latest known host, while season rows override it for historical editions. Sources: [official Laver Cup history](https://lavercup.com/history) and the [ATP 2026 Media Guide](https://www.atptour.com/-/media/files/media-guide/2026/2026-atp-media-guide-full-updated.pdf).
- The current and previous year pages are rechecked on each refresh; older calendar-year snapshots are retained. When an upstream source rate-limits or is unavailable, the builder keeps the last-known/checked-in year rows. Court Speed rows from a successful scrape replace matching event-season rows, while the reviewed CPI snapshot is retained for rows the parser cannot map.

The existing CPI/Surface Speed dataset refresh still validates and publishes `pace_dataset.json` and `data/latest.json` together. That workflow and its inputs are unchanged by Player Stats.

## Player Stats dataset

Player Stats is a completely separate `player_stats.json` asset. It does not extend or change the tournament `pace_dataset.json` schema, CPI readings, CALC/TA Surface Speed histories, live baseline or on-device live Surface Speed engine.

`tools/build_player_stats.py` downloads the TennisMyLife annual ATP match files and player roster into a temporary local cache, then writes `data/player_stats.json` and `app/src/main/assets/player_stats.json`. Its own workflow validates and commits only those two files. It does not invoke the existing CPI/Surface Speed builder or update `pace_dataset.json` / `data/latest.json`. Raw CSVs and raw ranking API responses are not committed.

The directory includes players with eligible ATP tour-level men's singles main-draw match results, plus any current Top-100 cohort member not found in that archive. It is not merely the ATP roster-only list. Qualifying, Challenger, Futures, doubles, Davis Cup and exhibitions are excluded. Seasons are calendar years from the tournament ID, not rolling 52-week windows. Played retirements count in W-L but their partial service/return data is excluded; walkovers, no-play defaults and abandoned matches are excluded.

Hold% is service games held divided by service games played. Service games held are derived from service games played less the breaks conceded (`bpFaced - bpSaved`). Break% is return games won divided by return games played; return games won are the opponent's breaks conceded and return games played are the opponent's service games played. Combined H+B is Hold% + Break%. Percentages use summed game counts and their exact denominators, not averages of match-level percentages. Incomplete or inconsistent match stats are not imputed.

Surface buckets retain clay, grass, carpet, hard overall, outdoor hard and indoor hard. The TML `indoor` field is normalized from its documented Yes/No values (and equivalent encodings); any other value remains unknown. Unknown hard-court environment is excluded from the specific indoor/outdoor splits but retained in the combined All hard aggregate and reported in coverage metadata.

Heights are stored as centimetres and shown in metres plus feet/inches. The player roster is preferred; a missing height may be filled by the most recent valid height in an eligible match record. Missing heights are not guessed.

The height-group cohorts are year-specific. The default Top 100 is still read from the same TML dated ranking API and date list as before. For each completed year from 1973 onward, Top 500/Top 1000 membership is read from the matching date in the public TML Rankings Archive CSV for that calendar year. The live current year uses the full KickServe ATP CSV `official_rank` field. KickServe's internal IDs are crosswalked to TML's stable player IDs through unique, normalized full-name keys; ambiguous or missing matches are left unresolved. Top 100 members omitted by that feed are restored from the same dated TML Top 100 snapshot, while rank conflicts still fail the refresh and the final Top 100 must match exactly. This avoids treating the TML dated API's 200-row response as a full Top 500/Top 1000. All later analytics join by stable TML player ID. Tied positions are preserved; rows with missing or placeholder IDs are counted separately and excluded from player-level calculations. Duplicate real player IDs are rejected. The selected date, provider and source URL for every year are stored in `meta.top100SeasonSnapshots` and `meta.top1000SeasonSnapshots`; player-level memberships are stored in `top100RanksBySeason` and `top1000RanksBySeason`. A snapshot that does not reach the requested cutoff is marked unavailable rather than being represented as a smaller cohort.

KickServe publishes its ATP CSV with an attribution requirement; the builder records the provider and data source URL, and the derived asset is subject to the provider's attribution and reuse terms; public repository visibility does not itself grant redistribution rights. See [KickServe open data](https://kick-serve.com/data/) and [PLAYER_STATS.md](PLAYER_STATS.md) for provenance and details.

The four height bands are under 183 cm, 183–190 cm, 191–194 cm and 195 cm or taller. Group rates pool service/return game counts; they are not unweighted means of player percentages. Group summaries report the number of cohort players, those with match results, those with valid service/return statistics, W-L, win percentage, matches and game denominators. Players with missing height are left unassigned.

The TennisMyLife source's public terms limit use to personal, educational and non-commercial research and do not authorise raw CSV redistribution/resale. This repository is public, so a committed generated Player Stats asset is publicly accessible; public visibility does not establish permission to redistribute the TML-derived data. Do not publish or distribute the derived asset or an APK containing it unless the source terms and any applicable permissions allow that use. See [DATA-LICENSES.md](../DATA-LICENSES.md) and [PLAYER_STATS.md](PLAYER_STATS.md) for the detailed method and source-use limitations.

The weekly Player Stats workflow updates the repository asset, not an already-installed APK. The screen reads the bundled snapshot and labels its build date; a newer ranking/match snapshot reaches the device with the next APK build. This is intentionally separate from the working CPI/Surface Speed refresh path.
